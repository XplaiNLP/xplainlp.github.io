Lisa Johanne Jacobs holds a Bachelor's degree in Germanistik and Ev. Theologie from the University of Kiel, and a Master's degree in Germanistik from the University of Leipzig. 
Since 2018, she has been involved in various projects related to antisemitism, including working on the "Antisemitismus 2.0" study at TU Berlin, as a research assistant at the University of Vienna, and at the RIAS Thüringen und Sachsen reporting office, as well as at CeMAS as a researcher. 
She is currently pursuing her PhD in Linguistics at TU Berlin. Her research focuses on antisemitism online and digital polarization.
